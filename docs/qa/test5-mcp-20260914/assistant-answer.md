The Bannockburn excerpt says that Bruce defeated Edward II in 1314. [C1]

For the Nairn retreat, Browne's account names Clanranald’s regiment, the French
piquets and Fitz-James’s horse as the rear. It describes the duke's advanced
guard as two hundred cavalry and the Argyleshire men. The Macdonalds of
Clanranald and Stewarts of Appin were ordered back to support the French. [C2]
