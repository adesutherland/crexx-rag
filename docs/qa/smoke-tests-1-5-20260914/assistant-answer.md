The Bannockburn excerpt states that Bruce defeated Edward II in 1314. [1]

In the Nairn passage, the duke’s advanced guard includes cavalry and the
Argyleshire men. The Macdonalds of Clanranald and Stewarts of Appin are ordered
back to support the French; this passage does not put those regiments in the
duke’s advanced guard. [2]

Following the returned graph lead resolves an index entry connecting Alaster
Macdonald with Dundee. That entry alone does not establish how Mackay and Dundee
were connected; it is a lead for further source inspection. [3]
