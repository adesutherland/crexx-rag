## Focused Proof

CTest `p2_09_donation_bundles` validates report coverage, adjacent docs and
metadata, safe unique manifest destinations, all required roles, existence and
hash identity for every staged file, and source fingerprints after staging.

It also compiles three Level-G minimized probes with and without optimization
and runs both VMs:

- SQLite opens an in-memory database and performs a typed parameter/result
  round-trip;
- provider validation accepts a local restricted request and rejects its
  hosted route before transport, making zero calls; and
- vector codec/search encodes two two-dimensional values and checks a bounded
  deterministic cosine result.

This is 12 probe cells. The isolated CTest passed in 7.22 seconds with 189,728
KiB maximum RSS. No provider call, credential read, normal-prefix write, or
donation submission occurred.
